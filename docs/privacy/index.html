Privacy policy

Updated 13 November 2026.

Escape Honey Traps is a free educational Android app. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. It helps adults recognise romance scams, sextortion, fake profiles, and workplace approaches, and it points to public helplines. This policy describes the app published on Google Play with the package name com.escapehoneytraps.app.

Data stays on your device

The app stores a small amount of information in local storage on your phone:

  • your theme choice (match the phone, light, or dark)
  • which lessons you have opened
  • whether you finished a lesson quiz, and your best quiz score for each lesson
  • the result of the last time you finished a practice drill

This information is not sent to the developer, to an analytics service, or to any other server. The app has no account, no login, and no cloud sync.

The red-flag checklist is not saved

Ticks on the Check tab stay in memory while that screen is open. They are not written to storage. Leave the screen or close the app and they are gone. The checklist has no free-text field.

What the app does not collect

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, messages, location, or payment details. It does not read your SMS, email, or social accounts. It does not include advertising, crash-reporting SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

The course works without a network connection. Lessons, drills, the checklist, and the written steps in Help still open if your phone has no data.

If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled on the phone. There is no push server and no notification token. The app stores whether each reminder is on, the time you chose, and when you last opened the app so a nudge can wait three days. A daily or Sunday reminder shows a hypothetical question only. It is not about your messages. Tapping it opens one practice question in the app. On Android 13 and newer, notification permission is requested only after you turn a reminder on.

The installed Android app declares an internet permission, which the Expo runtime includes, and a vibration permission so a tap can be felt. It does not use the internet permission to upload your progress, checklist, or anything you read. It does not ask for your photos, files, contacts, location, or permission to draw over other apps.

Calls and links you choose to open

Help contains buttons that open your phone’s dialer (for example 1930) or your browser (for example cybercrime.gov.in and stopncii.org). The app hands the link to the system. It does not receive what you later say to a helpline, a bank, or a website, and it does not upload screenshots or images.

Those organisations have their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and it should not be installed for a child. It discusses sextortion and intimate images in plain, non-explicit language. It does not allow anyone to upload photos.

How to delete what is stored

On the Learn tab, “Reset progress on this phone” deletes lesson and drill progress stored by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes its local storage, including the theme preference. There is no account to delete and no copy held by the developer.

Changes

If a later version stores or transmits anything new, this policy will change, and the Play listing will point at the updated policy before that version is what new users install.

Contact

There is no account to access, correct, or export. Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address published on the Google Play listing for Escape Honey Traps. Please do not send intimate images or account passwords to that address.
